Paper demo

Liquid on a sheet of paper that bends. Print the image below 25 cm wide (landscape A4 or US Letter), tap Start and point your phone at the page while it lies flat. Once it's found, pick it up and curl it: the liquid runs downhill along the paper, pools in the trough of the curl and pours off the edges. Tilt the page to pour it the other way, and tap the page to add more. The lines on the page are its contours.
