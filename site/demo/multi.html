Several targets & lazy content

Play the four videos on another screen. Tap Start on your phone and point it at the screen from about 30 cm away, so the clips fill the view. Each gets its own overlay, loaded only when it is found or nearly found.

Move closer to one clip to give it more tracking points. Turn away for more than five seconds: content is unloaded when room is needed. Favour a named target to change its tracking priority.
