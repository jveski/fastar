Audio timecode

Play a minute of timecode on another device, then tap Listen here. Phones listening to the same sound show the same set time; the small dot lights on each whole second.

The sweeps are at 19–20 kHz. Use speakers that reach 20 kHz; many laptop and phone speakers cannot. Start at low volume. Do not turn it up just because you cannot hear it. Microphone access needs HTTPS or localhost.

With image tracking enabled, point at this image on a second screen or printed 20 cm wide. The cube turns once every four seconds of the shared timeline, using the time each camera frame was captured, not the time it is rendered.
