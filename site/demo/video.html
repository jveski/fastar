Video demo

A cube on a video that never holds still. The whole picture churns, swirls and changes colour all the time, and nothing is sent between the two devices: the camera alone finds the screen and the moment the clip is at. Play the clip full screen on another device (double-click it), then tap Start on your phone and point it at the screen. Once the time is locked, the cube turns with the swirl beneath it, grows and shrinks with it, and changes colour with the clip.
