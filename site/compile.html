Target compiler

Turn images and short looping videos into a targets.bin. Enter the width each will be printed or shown at, so poses come out in metres. Videos are read frame by frame, which takes about as long as the clip plays. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

    Download targets.bin Try it in the demo

    Orange circles are detection features, teal squares are tracking points (for videos: on the first frame). Both should cover the image evenly.
